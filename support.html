In English

Have a question, found a bug, or want to suggest an idea? Email redpage1989@gmail.com.

To help us sort it out faster, please include:

  • your device model and iOS or Android version;
  • the app version;
  • what happened and what you did right before it;
  • a screenshot, if it helps.

Pro version. The Pro purchase is processed by the App Store or Google Play. If Pro did not activate, email us. Refunds are handled by Apple or Google under their own rules.

Your data. All projects, photos, and yarn records are stored only on your device, so we cannot view or restore them on our side. See the privacy policy for details.
